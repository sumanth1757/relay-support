Privacy policy

Effective September 30, 2026. Sumanth Karasala operates Relay Agent Studio. For privacy questions, email info@aximary.com.

How the app works

The iOS app connects to the Relay workspace service address that you configure. This release does not provide an Aximary-hosted Relay service. The app does not include advertising, tracking, or a developer-operated analytics service.

Workspace information

Your configured workspace service can store agent instructions, knowledge sources, conversation messages, citations, handoff status, and contact names or email addresses entered in conversations. This information is used to provide the workspace functions you request. The operator of the service controls its storage and access. If you enable the optional OpenAI response mode on that service, relevant message and source content can be sent to OpenAI to generate a reply. The default local knowledge mode does not call an AI provider.

Choices and deletion

You can edit or delete agents, sources, and conversations in the workspace. For deletion of a workspace or questions about a service you operate, contact its operator. For questions about this app or a support message you sent us, email info@aximary.com. Please do not send passwords or private conversation content in support email.

We will update this page before offering a developer-hosted workspace or changing the app's data practices.
